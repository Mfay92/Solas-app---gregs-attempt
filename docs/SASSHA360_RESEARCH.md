**Purpose:** Comprehensive documentation of SASSHA 360 housing management system to inform Solas CRM development.

## About SASSHA 360

**Official Site:** https://360.sassha.co.uk/

**Overview:**
- Dynamic SaaS Housing Management solution
- 360-degree view of all housing data
- Web browser + iPhone/Android apps
- Real-time CRM with SMS, Email, Phone integration
- Compliance features (Awaab's Law for mould/damp)

**Core Modules:**
- Tenancies
- Assets
- Maintenance
- Applications & Referrals
- Supported Housing
- Finance
- Rent & Benefits
- Repairs
- ASB (Anti-Social Behaviour)
- Complaints
